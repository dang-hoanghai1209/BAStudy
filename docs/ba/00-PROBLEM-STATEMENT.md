# Problem Statement

**Status:** Draft skeleton (VS-000). Needs validation against real or
interviewed sellers before being treated as final.

## The problem

Multi-channel E-commerce sellers can see their revenue easily, but not
their **true profit**. Revenue is reported per marketplace; cost of
goods, marketplace fees, shipping, advertising spend, discounts, and
refunds are scattered across different exports, spreadsheets, and
statements. As a result:

- High revenue is routinely mistaken for high profit
- Sellers cannot tell which SKUs or channels are actually profitable
- Manual reconciliation between "expected payout" and "actual payout
  received" is time-consuming and error-prone
- When a number looks wrong, there is no way to trace it back to its
  source row or the rule that produced it

## Who is affected

See `02-STAKEHOLDER-MAP.md` for the full list. Primarily: sellers/shop
owners and the analysts or accountants who support them.

## Why now / why this approach

Existing tools sellers use today (marketplace seller-center dashboards,
manual Excel) show revenue and raw fee line items, but do not compute a
trustworthy net profit per order that survives edits, refunds, and cost
changes over time. This is the gap SellerMargin targets.
